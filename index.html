Ilustrado Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Ilustrado (“the app”) is a quiz app for studying the life and works of José Rizal. It is made by TheLazyBattley (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and no ads, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your quiz history: the chapters you took, your answers, your scores and when you took each quiz. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Downloaded quiz content: the books, chapters and questions, kept so the app works offline.

You can delete your quiz history at any time with Clear History in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Information collected automatically

The app uses Google Firebase services, which collect some information automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics: how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened and when the app is first opened), an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. We use it to understand which features are used and to improve the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes, a crash report with technical details of the error, device model, operating system version, app version and a crash-reporting identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations: an installation identifier used to check whether new quiz content is available.
  • Cloud Firestore: when the app downloads quiz content, Google’s servers process your device’s IP address to deliver it.

This information is not used to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with other data about you.

Information you choose to send us

  • Book suggestions: the book title, author and, optionally, your school.
  • Answer reports: the question you reported, the issue you chose or typed, and the book and chapter it came from.

These submissions are not linked to your identity. They are stored in our Firebase database so we can review them and improve the questions. Treat them as non-private: please do not include personal information, such as your name or contact details, in any text you type.

How we share information

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. Google processes the information described above on our behalf as the provider of Firebase. You can read how Google handles this data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep information

  • Analytics data: kept for 2 months.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Suggestions and answer reports: kept until we have reviewed and acted on them, then deleted.
  • Data on your device: kept until you clear your history or uninstall the app.

Children

Ilustrado is made for high school and college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use Google’s infrastructure and limit who can change the app’s data.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your quiz history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data on your device.
  • To ask us to delete a suggestion or answer report you sent, email us with enough detail to find it (for example, the book title or question).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access or delete your information. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version at this address. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.
